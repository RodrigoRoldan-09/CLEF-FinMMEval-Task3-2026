    \begin{abstract}
    In this work we describe a multimodal pipeline that fuses textual news, temporal price sequences, and numerical scalars into a single coherent prompt for a quantized language model. The decision model, based on \texttt{Phi-4-mini-instruct} in 4-bit NF4 quantization, is trained via a compact two-stage curriculum that combines supervised fine-tuning (SFT) with Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) under a strict 12\,GB VRAM budget. We detail the exact hyperparameters, the asymmetric reward matrix that breaks the Nash equilibrium of inaction, and the action-extraction mechanism that rescues truncated tokens. The resulting agent achieves a cumulative return of 90.67\% on our validation set, and we provide a complete forensic reconstruction of the training protocol so that independent researchers can reproduce the system on commodity hardware.
    \end{abstract}
